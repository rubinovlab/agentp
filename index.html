AGENT-P

Association of Gene Expression to Neuroimaging Traits Pipeline

An integrated, comprehensive, and accessible pipeline for transcriptome-wide association studies (TWAS) of neuroimaging phenotypes. TWAS predicts how much each gene is expressed in the brain from a person's genotypes (genetically regulated gene expression, or GREx), and tests whether this predicted expression is associated with a trait, such as the volume of a brain region.

AGENT-P integrates four popular TWAS methods for eight cortical and subcortical regions, and can be readily extended to include additional models of gene expression and brain regions. It runs TWAS in two ways:

TWASInputAGENT-P class
Summary-levelGWAS summary statistics of the traitSummaryTWAS
Genotype-levelgenotypes, covariates, and trait values of individual subjectsIndividualTWAS

AGENT-P can also separately run a GWAS with REGENIE, combine the results of all models of gene expression into one p-value per gene as meta-analysis TWAS (ACAT), and make Manhattan and Miami plots.

Try it online

The example notebook runs in Google Colab, with nothing to install. It runs summary-level and genotype-level TWAS on 2,000 example subjects with the JTI models of caudate gene expression and amygdala volume, then shows the top genes and Manhattan and Miami plots.

Open the example in Colab, then choose Runtime → Run all. The Example page explains each step.
